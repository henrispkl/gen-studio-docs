Paint a model's texture

Repaint a model's texture from a picture with Hunyuan3D-Paint, then bake it onto the game mesh. The mesh, its UVs and its rig stay as they are.

Before you start​

  • A prop or character with a Game mesh take, made or imported.
  • About 21 GB of free graphics memory, and 4 to 7 minutes. Paint is one of the heaviest steps.
  • The Paint model's weights. If they're missing, the app asks before it downloads them.
  • Hunyuan3D-Paint has a non-commercial licence that excludes the EU, the UK and South Korea. Gen Studio records it in the take.

Paint the whole model​

  1. Open the asset. Press Run next: Paint on the Game mesh, or add Paint with the + on the rail. It's optional, and Run to end skips it.
  2. Leave Reference image empty to paint from the concept in use (and, for a character, its turnaround). Or pick your own picture.
  3. Press New take.

The take is the game mesh with the new texture. On a character, the Rig step is built from the painted mesh, so it turns stale if it already ran.

Paint only part of it​

  1. In Only paint, name the part as you'd point at it: the face, the boots. With a part named, Build on Take N paints on that take, so parts stack; a whole repaint starts from the step before.
  2. Lower Paint strength below 1 to keep some of the model's own texture under the paint.
  3. Press New take. The rest keeps its texture.

To mark the part by hand in 3D, use Edit part of a model.

Repaint in another style​

This is how to give an imported model a new look.

  1. Click Concept on the rail and pick a take (for an imported model, its render).
  2. Press Restyle…, describe the look in What it should look like (a weathered bronze knight) and press Restyle. You get a new concept take with the same outline.
  3. Use that take, then run Paint.

From the command line, to paint another take of the Game mesh or the Rig as a new take of that same step:

gen retake knight concept --set control=renders/knight.png --prompt "a weathered bronze knight"
gen retake knight retopo --set paint_from=2
gen retake knight skin --set paint_from=1 --set region="the boots" --set mix=0.7

paint_from names the take to paint. image, region and mix are the form's fields.

What you get​

New maps baked onto your mesh's own UVs. Which ones depends on the target look: base colour at PS1 and PS2, plus roughness at PS3, plus metallic at PS4. At PS1 and PS2 the base colour also gets a palette, the same readability pass as the original texture.

Troubleshooting​

  • "Paint needs a Game mesh take first": run the steps up to Game mesh.
  • Out of memory: close other GPU programs.
  • It looks wrong from behind: the concept shows only the front. Characters use their turnaround too; for a prop, give it a reference picture that shows more.
