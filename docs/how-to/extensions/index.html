Extensions on and off

Turn a pack of models on or off, so its steps, asset kinds and downloads appear or go away.

Most of Gen Studio's steps come from extensions: packs of steps, each with its own models and settings (gen.concept for concept art, gen.ace-step for music). Most are on. The anime pack, gen.anime, its add-on gen.anime-noobai and the pixel art and chiptune pack, gen.pixel, ship off.

In the app​

  1. Open Settings (Ctrl+,) → Extensions.
  2. Search, or filter by category. Each row has a switch and its library, size on disk and description. Pick one to see its steps and models.
  3. Click the switch.
  4. Restart the app. Changes apply on the next start.
  5. If you turned one on, install its models under Settings → Models. Or don't: they download the first time a step needs them, after the app asks.

From the command line​

gen ext list                 # every extension, on or off, and its stages
gen ext enable gen.anime
gen install weights --group anime
gen ext disable gen.anime

The switches are stored in the [extensions] table of ~/.config/gen-studio/config.toml.

What "off" means​

An off extension adds nothing: no steps, no asset kinds, no fields, no downloads. Assets you made with it keep their folders and takes, but they stay out of the app until you turn the pack back on. Turning a pack off deletes nothing.

Add-ons​

An add-on offers another model for its parent's steps. gen.anime-noobai adds models to the anime pack's steps. It's active only while its parent is on, and the step then shows a Model choice beside Profile.

Your own extensions​

  • Put an extension folder in extensions/ of the Gen Studio folder, or in <data folder>/extensions/.
  • gen new extension me.foley --kind worker scaffolds one.
  • gen ext validate checks every installed manifest, pin and environment; gen ext validate <folder> checks one.

How to write one is in the repository's AGENTS.md. Every installed extension has a page in the wiki.

Tips​

  • Turn off packs you don't use, to keep Settings → Models and New asset short.
  • gen update writes gen.lock from the installed extensions. gen restore installs exactly what it pins on another machine.
