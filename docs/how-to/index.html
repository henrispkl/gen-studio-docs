How-to guides

One task per page. New to the app? Start with the tutorials.

Takes and steps​

  • Retake and compare: make more takes and keep the best.
  • Reroll a take: a new seed that replaces a take.
  • Run the stale steps: rebuild what's out of date.
  • Add steps and paths: add a step, branch from one, delete one.
  • Write better prompts: the writing helper, the prompt guide, your prompts and presets.
  • Use the queue: see, pause and cancel what's running.

Pictures​

  • Retouch a picture: repaint an area you mark by hand.
  • Vary a region: redraw one part of a concept by naming it.

3D​

  • Paint a model's texture: repaint it from a picture.
  • Edit part of a model: repaint or remake only what you mark.
  • Import your own models: bring in an FBX, OBJ, GLB, VRM or .blend.
  • Cloth presets: make capes, skirts and long hair swing.

Sound​

  • The mixer and layers: stack sounds and shape them.
  • Clean up a recording: noise, clicks and a dull top end.
  • Stems and MIDI: split a track and edit its notes.

Godot​

  • Export to Godot: what you get, and where it goes.
  • The Godot dock: the queue and Import inside the editor.

Bigger jobs​

  • Batches overnight: many jobs from one file.
  • The queue on your phone: watch and steer from the sofa.

Assets and disk​

  • Find free assets: search free libraries, with licences.
  • Extensions on and off: which packs of models you have.
  • Move your data: the data folder, and what uses the space.
  • Libraries and drives: models on the drive you choose.
