Make more takes and keep the best

You get several takes of one step, see them side by side, and choose the one the next step uses.

Before you start​

You need an asset whose step has run at least once (see your first prop). The examples use the sample prop lantern.

  • In the app
  • Command line
  1. Open the asset and click the step on the rail.
  2. Change what you want in the inputs on the left: the prompt, the Profile, a slider. Leave them alone for the same settings with a new seed.
  3. Press Retake in the step's title bar (R). On the New take card in the strip, the same button reads New take. The new take appears in the strip while it runs. The take in use stays in use, so nothing after it changes yet.
  4. To compare, hover a take card and tick the box in its corner, or Ctrl-click it. Pick two or more, then press Compare N in the title bar.
  5. Click the take you want, then press Use this take in the title bar (U). When there is a step after it, the button reads Use and run with that step's name, and starts it too.

The steps built from the old take are now stale.

gen retake lantern concept --prompt "a brass lantern with green glass panes"
gen retake lantern retopo --set triangles=2000 --profile balanced
gen status lantern                 # every step's takes, the one in use, stale steps
gen promote lantern concept 3      # use Take 3 (v003)
gen discard lantern concept 2      # Take 2 to the project's .trash/

--set takes any field of the step's form by its key. The keys are in steps and fields. A take from gen retake is kept, but not used until you promote it.

Variations​

  • Your own seed: each take gets a new random seed while the shuffle button beside Seed is on. Turn it off to set one.
  • Without opening the step: right-click the step on the rail and choose Retake.
  • Use without running on: hover a take in the strip and press its check mark, or double-click it, or right-click it and choose Use this take. The small arrow on Use and run goes on from the take already in use instead.
  • What Compare shows: the same output of each take side by side, with its stats. 3D views turn and zoom together. Sounds stop the others when one plays, and they are matched in loudness, so you compare the sound and not the level.
  • Mark the good ones: right-click a take for Favourite and a rating from 1 to 5.
  • Throw one away: Discard… moves a take to the project's .trash/. You can't discard the take in use.
  • Reuse settings: Advanced under the inputs has Save as preset for values you use again.
  • Change one thing per take. Then you know what made the difference.
