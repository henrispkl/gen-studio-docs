Cloth presets

Make a cape, a skirt or long hair swing in Godot, and choose how it moves.

Gen Studio adds bone chains down the parts that hang. In Godot, a SpringBoneSimulator3D swings them while the character moves. Nothing is baked into the clips, so the cloth reacts to whatever the character does in the game.

Long hair on a character​

  1. Open the character and click Rig on the rail.
  2. Set Cloth:
    • Auto (the default) adds chains when the description mentions hair: long hair, ponytail, braid.
    • Hair always adds them. None never does.
  3. To change how it moves, open Advanced and set Cloth material.
  4. Press Retake.

Capes and skirts aren't part of the character. Make them as wearables.

A cape, skirt or coat as a wearable​

  1. Make a Wearable (under Characters & bodies in New asset), or open one.
  2. On its Design step, set Cloth:
    • Auto reads the piece's words: cape, cloak, mantle swing as a cape; skirt, dress, robe, coat, kilt, tabard, gown as a skirt; hair, ponytail, braid as hair; tail, ribbon, scarf, tassel as a tail.
    • Or pick Cape, Skirt, Hair, Tail or None.
  3. Set Cloth material under Advanced if Auto isn't right.
  4. Run the pipeline to Skin. The chains are added and checked there.

Cloth material​

MaterialStiffnessDragGravityFor
cloth0.60.41.0capes, skirts
leather1.40.50.8coats, belts
hair0.90.30.6hair, tails
chain0.40.21.6chains, heavy jewellery

Stiffness springs the chain back to its rest shape, drag damps the swing, gravity pulls it down. Auto uses cloth for capes and skirts, hair for hair and tails.

gen retake herbalist skin --set cloth=hair --set cloth_material=chain
gen retake red_cape wear_design --set cloth=cape --set cloth_material=leather

What you get​

  • A wearable's Skin take swings the chains in headless Godot before it's kept. If they stretch more than 5 %, the take fails and says why.
  • With a display, the take also has a cloth.webp preview of the swing. The numbers are in wear.json under cloth_check.
  • Export writes a <piece>.cloth.json beside the wearable, and for a character's own hair a <name>.cloth.json and a ready <name>_cloth.tscn.

Tips​

  • In Godot, Wearables.cloth() sets up the simulator from a .cloth.json (see Godot scripts).
  • Capsule colliders on the hips, spine, chest and thighs, scaled to the character, keep the cloth out of the body.
