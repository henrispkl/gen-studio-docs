Gen Studio

Gen Studio makes game assets on your own computer, on one GPU: concept art, 3D models, rigs, animation, sound, music, voices, text, textures, effects, shaders and anime art. The results go into your Godot project.

Generation runs on your machine; the network is used to download models and to search free libraries. Every result keeps a record of how it was made, so you can make it again or change one step.

In the app​

Help → Help… (F1) has the reference side of these pages: the step you're on, asset kinds, steps, concepts, troubleshooting, What's new, the keyboard shortcuts and a search. The step-by-step tutorials run from there, pointing at each control. Guides and tutorials are read on this website; each Help page links to its full version.

Meet Rin​

The elf with the eye patch on these pages is Rin, Gen Studio's mascot. Every picture of her was made in the app: an Anime character with her own LoRA, then shots with the rim light. The anime tutorial shows how.
