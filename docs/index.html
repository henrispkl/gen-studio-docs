Gen Studio

Gen Studio makes game assets on your own computer, on one GPU: concept art, 3D models, rigs, animation, textures, effects, shaders, sound, music, voices, text, anime art and pixel art. The results go into your Godot project. It's for people making a game alone or in a small team, who want every asset to keep a record of how it was made.

Generation runs on your machine. The network is used to download models and to search free asset libraries.

What you need: Linux and an NVIDIA GPU (built and tested on an RTX 4090 with 24 GB; many steps want 16 to 24 GB of graphics memory), 80 GB of disk for the Minimal install, 266 GB for Recommended or 358 GB for Full, Godot 4.7, and Blender on your PATH. The details are in Set up Gen Studio.

What do you want to make?​

A propFrom a sentence to a low-poly model in Godot.A character that movesConcept, 3D, a skeleton, then clips.Textures, shaders, effectsA seamless tile, a shader that compiles, a flipbook.Sound and musicSound effects, a looping track, a composed piece.Voices and wordsA voice for a character, dialogue, item text.Anime charactersA character sheet, shots, expressions, a 2D rig.Pixel art and chiptuneTrue pixels on a palette, and chip music.OutfitsArmour and clothes fitted to a rigged character.
