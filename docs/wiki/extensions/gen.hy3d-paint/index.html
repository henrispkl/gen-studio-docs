gen.hy3d-paint

At a glance
Idgen.hy3d-paint
Version0.1.0
Category3d
On by defaultyes
VRAM21 GB
Installgen install weights (groups: hy3d-paint)

Paint texture: Hunyuan3D-Paint 2.1 paints albedo, roughness and metallic onto a model you have (or made) from one reference image; the core bakes them back onto the original mesh, keeping its topology and rig. ~21 GB of VRAM. Tencent Hunyuan non-commercial licence (no EU/UK/KR).
