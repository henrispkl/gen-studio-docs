Shot

At a glance

One shot of a defined anime character (or up to four): framing, pose, background and expression, drawn by Anima with the character's tags and LoRA.

How it works​

The character's sheet tags (and LoRA, once trained) lead the prompt; the Framing, Pose and Background choices add theirs; your Shot text says the rest. A transparent shot is cut out by ToonOut, then ViTMatte redraws the edge so thin outlines on hair tips and fingers stay (advanced Cut-out: ToonOut only gives the older, softer edge). Close framings are drawn waist-up and cropped around the face, then given a detail pass. At Balanced and Quality the shot is finished: an AnimeSharp upscale to 1.5×, a light refine, and a face pass. With three or more characters each is redrawn in its own place (Layout) and the faces are counted, one per place.

Use it​

  • App: open the asset and click Shot on the rail (or add it with the +), set the form on the left and press New take. Pick the take you like and press Use this take.
  • CLI: gen retake <asset> anime_shot, with --set field=value for any field below; gen run-to <asset> anime_shot runs up to it.
  • Claude Code: fields shows the form, retake makes a take, look shows it, use picks it.

Tips​

  • Expressions and actions go in Shot; the look is already in the character's tags.
  • For a group, describe what they do together; draw a Layout with coloured boxes to place them.

Anima (WAI-Anima) (ultra_fast, fast, balanced, quality): One shot of a defined character: their tags (and LoRA) come first, then what you write here. Framing, pose and background are choices in the form; this field says the rest.

Structure: [expression] → [action or pose details] → [outfit changes] → [place, when the background is a scene] → [light].

  • Expressions and actions are what the Shot is for: 'laughing, closed eyes, arms up', 'serious, holding sword, fighting stance'.
  • An outfit change goes here too ('winter coat, scarf'); the character's usual outfit is already in their tags.
  • With Background set to A scene, say the place here: 'tavern, indoors, warm lighting'.
  • Pose choices in the form are written into the prompt for you; say finer details here ('left hand on hip').
  • Close framings (Chest-up, Head, Eyes) are drawn waist-up, then cropped around the face and given a detail pass.

Avoid:

  • Repeating the character's look: it's already in their tags.
  • Framing tags: the Framing choice sets them.

Examples:

  • laughing, closed eyes, arms up
  • smug, crossed arms, looking at viewer
  • serious, holding sword, fighting stance, wind
  • embarrassed, blush, looking away, hand on own cheek

NoobAI-XL (ultra_fast, fast, balanced, quality): One shot of a defined character with NoobAI-XL: their tags (and LoRA) come first, then the tags you write here. Framing, pose (an OpenPose skeleton with NoobAI) and background are choices in the form.

Structure: [expression] → [action] → [outfit changes] → [place, when the background is a scene] → [light].

  • Expression and action tags: 'laughing, closed eyes, arms up', 'serious, holding sword, fighting stance'.
  • With NoobAI the Pose choice is drawn from an OpenPose skeleton, so pose tags here are for details only ('hand on hip').
  • An outfit change goes here ('winter coat, scarf'); the usual outfit is already in the character's tags.
  • With Background set to A scene, tag the place: 'tavern, indoors, warm lighting'.

Avoid:

  • Repeating the character's look, or framing tags (the Framing choice sets them).
  • Sentences: NoobAI reads tags.

Examples:

  • laughing, closed eyes, arms up
  • smug, crossed arms, looking at viewer
  • serious, holding sword, fighting stance, wind
  • embarrassed, blush, looking away, hand on own cheek

Troubleshooting​

  • Two characters blend: add a Layout, or retake; each figure is redrawn in its own place.
  • The cut-out loses a hand: retake on Transparent (magenta screen) if the outfit is green.
