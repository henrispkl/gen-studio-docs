Claude Codefields, retake, look, use

The Pixelate step of pixel_icon.

Use it​

  • App: open the asset and click Pixelate on the rail (or add it with the +), set the form on the left and press New take. Pick the take you like and press Use this take.
  • CLI: gen retake <asset> pixelate_icon, with --set field=value for any field below; gen run-to <asset> pixelate_icon runs up to it.
  • Claude Code: fields shows the form, retake makes a take, look shows it, use picks it.

Settings​

FieldTypeDefaultWhat it does
Size sizechoice (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, auto)32The longest side, in real pixels. 48 keeps most drawings readable; 32 suits simple subjects. Auto uses the drawing's own fake-pixel grid when it clearly has one, else 48.
Colours coloursint 2.0…64.016How many colours the free palette keeps; small bright accents survive. A fixed palette ignores this.
Palette palettechoice (free, pico8, nes, gb, db16, db32, endesga32)freeA console's or a well-known artist's colours, matched by how alike they look (OKLab).
Clean outline outlineboolTrueWhen the drawing has a dark outline, make it continuous and one pixel wide. No outline is added to a drawing without one.
Background backgroundchoice (cut, keep)cutCut it out: the flat background around the subject becomes transparent and the sprite is cropped to it.
Outline strength dark_sharefloat 0.1…0.90.4How much of a pixel must be dark for it to become outline: lower keeps thin lines but thickens them. (advanced)
