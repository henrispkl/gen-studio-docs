Your first prop

You'll make a copper still: concept art, a 3D model, a game mesh, then put it in Godot. About 10 minutes on a 24 GB GPU, most of it waiting for the models.

Before you start​

  • Gen Studio is set up, and gen doctor says everything is OK. The Minimal tier (80 GB) is enough for this tutorial.
  • You have a Godot 4 project somewhere on disk.

New to the screens? Read the tour first.

1. Make a project​

A project is a folder that holds your assets, usually one per game.

On Home, under Projects, press New project…. Pick a Target look (the era your game looks like, it sets the polygon budget), then an empty folder. Pick PS2 or PS3 for this one.

The target look and the default profile live in gen-project.toml in that folder, and you can change them in Settings → Project.

2. Describe it​

Press All kinds… on Home (or Ctrl+N) and pick 3D prop under Props & scenery. The form shows the steps it will run.

Write what you want in Prompt:

a squat copper alembic still with a round belly on an iron tripod, green patina at the seams

Press the sparkle beside Asset name for a name from the prompt, or type copper_still. Say what it's made of and how worn it is. The camera and the plain background are added for you.

Press Generate.

On the command line, from the project folder:

gen make prop "a squat copper alembic still with a round belly on an iron tripod, green patina at the seams" --asset copper_still

3. Look at the takes​

The asset opens with one step on the rail: Concept. It draws a picture.

Each try is a take. Don't like it? Press Retake; the old one stays, side by side in the strip. Click a take to see it, and Use this take to build on it.

Fix it here

Make two or three concept takes before you go on. Fixing a shape in the picture is quicker than fixing it in 3D.

4. Run to the end​

Press Run to end in the toolbar. New steps appear on the rail as they start:

  • Prepped cut-out cuts the picture out.
  • Mesh builds a dense 3D model from it.
  • Game mesh rebuilds that inside your target look's polygon budget and bakes the texture.

To go one step at a time instead, press Run next in the bar above the take.

5. Look at it​

Open Game mesh, then Open large (or double-click). Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom, press 3 for the wireframe and 7 for the UV checker. The side panel shows the triangles against the budget.

Not happy? Change a value in the form, or go back and use another concept take. The steps after it turn stale, and Re-run stale rebuilds them.

6. Put it in Godot​

Press Export to Godot in the toolbar. The first time, pick your Godot project's folder.

gen export copper_still ~/your-godot-game

The .glb lands in res://assets/gen/copper_still/. Drag it into a scene.

What you learned​

  • A project, a target look and a profile.
  • Steps, takes, and the take in use.
  • Run to end, and exporting to Godot.
