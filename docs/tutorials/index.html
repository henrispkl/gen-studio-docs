Tutorials

Learn by doing, start to finish. Each one ends with something in your game. New here? Do them in this order.

Your first propYou'll make a copper still: concept art, a 3D model, a game mesh, then put it in Godot. About 10 minutes on a 24 GB GPU, most of it waiting for the models. A character, from concept to GodotA herbalist with a rig and a walk cycle, in Godot. About 15 minutes at the Fast profile. A clip from your phoneFilm yourself doing a move, and put it on your character. About 5 minutes, plus filming. Sound for a gameA sound effect, a few voiced lines and a looping theme for one small scene: a herbalist's shop. About 10 minutes. A shader from a descriptionDescribe a shader in a sentence and get a .gdshader that compiles in Godot, with sliders for what you want to tweak. About 2 minutes at Fast. A base body and an outfitDress one body in many outfits, and swap them at runtime in Godot. About an hour at Fast for the body and four pieces; most of that is the model and rig steps. An anime character with sprites and a 2D rigRin, the elf on these pages, was made this way. Define a character once, take shots of her, then make a visual novel sprite set and a 2D rig for Godot. About 30 minutes without the LoRA, about an hour with it. Working with Claude Code over MCPClaude Code can run Gen Studio for you: make assets, look at the takes, pick, retake and export.
