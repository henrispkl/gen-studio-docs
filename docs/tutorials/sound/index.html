Sound and music

You give the village its sound: two sound effects with variants, a looping theme, and a waltz written note by note that you can edit bar by bar. Then you clean one sound up and run it through effects. About 15 minutes.

You'll make:

  • bottle_clink and door_creak, sound effects with several variants each
  • village_theme, a 60-second track that loops
  • festival_waltz, a composed piece with a MIDI file for a DAW

You'll learn:

  • variants, and picking the ones that go on
  • what pins a track: length, BPM, key
  • the composer and its editor
  • the optional Clean up and Effects steps

Before you start​

  • Do your first prop, for takes and export.
  • Headphones or speakers. Sounds are matched in loudness, so you compare the sound and not the level.

Sound effects​

  1. Press All kinds…, then Sound and Sound effect.

  2. Write this in Sound:

    two glass potion bottles clinking together

  3. Set Length (seconds) to 1.5. Leave Variants at 4.

  4. Type bottle_clink in Asset name and press Generate.

  5. Play each variant in the take (Space plays and pauses). Press Pick this one on the ones you keep. The button then reads Picked.

  6. Make door_creak the same way, at 2.5 seconds:

    an old wooden door creaking open

Checkpoint

two sound assets, each with one take of four variants and at least one picked.

Variants are a round robin: the same sound, each nudged a little in pitch and level, so it doesn't sound the same twice in your game. Describe the source and the action, and keep it to one event. The steps after this one go on with the variants you picked.

A theme that loops​

  1. Press All kinds…, then Sound and Music.

  2. Write tags in Style: genre, mood, instruments.

    cozy mountain village theme, hammered dulcimer, pizzicato strings and a soft flute, warm and hopeful, instrumental

  3. Set Length (seconds) to 60 and BPM to 96. Leave Seamless loop on.

  4. Open Advanced and set Key to G major.

  5. Type village_theme in Asset name and press Generate.

  6. Play the take, then press L to loop it and listen across the join.

Checkpoint

the take's waveform has its loop points marked, and the join is hard to hear.

Seamless loop finds a loop point and cuts the track to it, on whole bars. Leave Lyrics empty for an instrumental. From here, Add step offers Stems (drums, bass, vocals and the rest) and then MIDI: see stems and MIDI.

# village_theme.yaml, run with: gen run village_theme.yaml
asset: village_theme
kind: music
seed: 12
prompt: cozy mountain village theme, hammered dulcimer, pizzicato strings and a soft flute, warm and hopeful, instrumental
bpm: 96
duration: 60
keyscale: G major

A piece written in notes​

The Music step makes audio. The composer writes notes: rules and tables, no model, nothing on the GPU, done in seconds. You get a .mid for a DAW and a preview to hear.

  1. Press All kinds…, then Sound and Composed music.

  2. Set Style to Gentle town: a light major tune for flute, clarinet and piano.

  3. Type festival_waltz in Asset name and press Generate. The seed picks the key, tempo, form and ending. With Seed at 14 and Random seed off you get the piece on these pages.

  4. Play the take. Then press Edit on it to open the composer's editor.

  5. Click an instrument's notes in one bar, then press New idea. It's a fresh draw of only what you picked, listed under Takes with the changed notes lit. Pick the one to keep, or go back to Current.

  6. Press Save as new take. Nothing changes until you do, and the take you opened stays as it was.

Checkpoint

the Compose step has two takes: the first draw, and yours with one bar changed.

Rework rhythm and Rework pitches vary notes that are already there, and Notes is a piano roll where you draw your own. Edit a composed piece covers the editor.

Clean up and Effects​

Both steps are optional and sit after the sound. Try them on the door.

  1. Open door_creak and press Add step at the end of the rail. Pick Clean up.

  2. Leave Strength on Normal and Shimmer on Auto, and press New take. Each sound is measured first, and only what the measurements show gets fixed.

  3. Press Add step again and pick Effects. The middle of this step is the mixer.

  4. Open Quick setup in the inputs, choose a Room, and press Fill the rack. What you hear is live.

  5. Press New take to render the rack into a take.

Checkpoint

the door's rail reads Sound effect, Clean up, Effects. The Effects take sounds like the door in a room.

Role on the Effects step sets the loudness the sound is delivered at and whether it's mono (placed in 3D) or stereo. More in clean up a recording and the mixer and layers.

What you learned​

  • A sound effect is several variants, and you pick which go on.
  • Music is audio from tags, with loop points. Composed music is notes from rules, which you can edit.
  • Clean up fixes, Effects shapes, and neither changes the take they started from.

If it went wrong​

  • A sound has hiss or a metallic shimmer: clean up a recording.
  • The loop's join is easy to hear: retake the Music step.
  • "Needs a download": see troubleshooting.
  • Anything else: troubleshooting.
