A clip from your phone

You film yourself doing a move and put it on your character as a clip. About 5 minutes, plus the filming.

You'll make:

  • a clip on the courier, from your own video

You'll learn:

  • how to film for motion capture
  • where a video goes in New clip
  • what makes a clip loop and stay in place

Before you start​

  • A rigged character: the courier from a character that moves.
  • The motion-capture model: GEM-X at Balanced, SAM 3D Body at Quality. The app asks before it downloads one.

Film it​

  1. Stand the phone somewhere still, with your whole body in frame. Use a plain background if you can.
  2. Film about 5 seconds. Side-on for walks, front-on for gestures.
  3. Wear fitted clothes. Baggy ones that hide the knees track worse.
  4. Trim the video to the move. The start and the end matter for loops.
Checkpoint

a short video file with one move in it, head to feet visible throughout.

Make the clip​

  1. Copy the video into your project folder, for example as refs/wave.mp4.
  2. Open the courier and press ⋮, then New clip.
  3. Type wave in Clip name.
  4. Type the video's path, relative to the project, in Or a video file (motion capture): refs/wave.mp4.
  5. Press Generate.
Checkpoint

a new clip row appears on the rail. Its step tracks your body in the video, then retargets the motion to the courier's skeleton.

From the command line it's a job file:

# wave.yaml, run with: gen run wave.yaml
asset: courier
kind: mocap
clip: wave
video: refs/wave.mp4

Check it​

  1. Click the clip on the rail and press Open large.
  2. Press play, or drag the timeline. Bones shows the skeleton.
  3. If the feet slide or an arm is lost, film again with the whole body in frame, and retake.
Checkpoint

the courier does your move, on the spot.

A clip loops and stays in place by default: the end meets the start, and the hips don't wander. To change that, make the clip from a job file with loop: false or in_place: false.

What you learned​

  • Motion capture from a plain video.
  • What makes a clip loop and stay in place.

If it went wrong​

  • The clip slides, or the feet float: see troubleshooting.
  • "Needs a download": see troubleshooting.
