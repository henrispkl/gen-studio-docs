An anime character with sprites and a 2D rig

Rin, the elf on these pages, was made this way. Define a character once, take shots of her, then make a visual novel sprite set and a 2D rig for Godot. About 30 minutes without the LoRA, about an hour with it.

Before you start​

The anime pack is off by default. Turn it on in Settings → Extensions → gen.anime, restart the app, and install its models:

gen install weights --group anime

1. Define the character​

Press All kinds…, then Anime character under Anime. Describe them in Danbooru tags: who they are, hair, eyes, build and outfit. Rin's are:

1girl, solo, elf, pointy ears, blonde hair, very long hair, black eyepatch, red eyes, mole under mouth,
burgundy vest, sleeveless, white fur collar, grey turtleneck, black belt, dark brown shorts,
black gloves, brown thigh boots

You get a full-body sheet on white: the definition every shot copies.

2. Her own LoRA (optional)​

Tags keep a character close, not exact. The optional LoRA step trains a small add-on for the drawing model on pictures of her. It takes 20 to 40 minutes and the whole GPU. Every shot of her uses it from then on. Rin's was trained on 120 of 320 pictures, the ones that matched her design.

3. Shots​

Make a Shot of a character (under Anime): pick the character, a Framing (knee-up is the usual sprite), a Pose, and a Background: Transparent for a sprite, or A scene. Write the rest in Shot: smile, looking at viewer, or sitting, reading book, library.

Up to four characters fit in one shot. With three or more, each is redrawn in its own place (Layout), so they don't swap clothes.

4. The rim light​

The optional Rim light step after a shot paints a bright, thin rim on the edges facing a light, like fighting-game key art. Pick the Rim colour, Light from and Strength (1 to 5). Keep only the rim colour drops anything else the relight painted.

The take shows the lit picture, the raw relight and the cut-out.

The result changes with the seed. If the rim is faint, Reroll the take. About one in four or five is a great rim.

gen retake rin_wave anime_rim --set strength=3 --set filter=true
gen reroll rin_wave anime_rim

5. A sprite set​

Make a Sprite or portrait set from the knee-up shot: every expression you ask for, each with talking and blinking frames, a faceset, and files for Ren'Py (.rpy) and Godot (sprite_frames.tres with portrait.gd).

6. A 2D rig​

Make a 2D rig (cut-out animation) from the same transparent shot. It's split into parts (hair, face, arms, legs, torso), the hidden areas are filled in, and you get a Godot scene with bones and three animations: idle breathing, a sway and a head tilt. The back hair swings.

Open <name>.tscn in Godot. The AnimationPlayer plays idle on start.

What you learned​

  • A character defined once, then shot in any framing.
  • Her own LoRA for an exact look.
  • The rim light, sprite sets and 2D rigs.
