Glossary

The words Gen Studio uses. New here? Read the tour first.

Asset​

One thing you make: a prop, a character, a sound, a track, a texture, a shader. It's a folder in assets/<name>/.

Asset kind​

What sort of asset it is (prop, character, body, wearable, music, anime_shot). The kind picks its steps. All of them: asset kinds.

Pipeline​

The steps an asset kind goes through, left to right: a prop's Concept, Mesh, Game mesh; a character's Concept, Turnaround, Mesh, Game mesh, Rig, then clips.

Step​

One stage of a pipeline, with its own form and its own takes. In the CLI and in folders it goes by its id: Game mesh is retopo, Rig is skin. Every step and its fields: steps reference.

Take​

One try at a step. Take 3 in the app, v003 in folders and on the CLI. Takes stay until you discard them.

In use​

The take the next steps are built from. One per step.

Stale​

A step built from a take that's no longer in use. Re-run stale or Run to end rebuilds it, except a take built on another take, which you rebuild by hand.

Review (gate)​

A step that stops for you to check before the rest runs: a character's Rig, a wearable's Design. Continue runs on.

Path (branch)​

Another route from a step, on its own row of the rail, named b1, b2. See add steps and paths.

Clip​

An animation on a character's rig, made from text or from a video. Each clip has its own row on the rail (clips/walk/motion).

Profile​

How much time a step may take: Ultra fast, Fast, Balanced, Quality. It picks the model and its settings. See profiles and target looks.

Target look (fidelity)​

The era your game looks like: PS1, PS2, PS3, PS4. It sets polygon and texture budgets and frame rates. The app says target look; the CLI and gen-project.toml say fidelity. See fidelity targets.

Model choice​

On a step more than one model can run, which one runs it.

Extension​

A pack of steps, models and pipelines in extensions/<author.name>/, described by gen-extension.toml. Turn them on and off in Settings → Extensions.

Add-on​

An extension that adds another model to another extension's steps (extends = "gen.anime").

Weights​

A model's files, pinned by exact revision and hash. gen install weights downloads them. All of them: models and licences.

Tier​

How much gen install weights downloads: Minimal, Recommended or Full. On demand models download the first time a step needs them.

Library​

A folder on any drive where models and their environments live. See libraries and drives.

Provenance​

The record in every take's meta.json of how it was made, so it can be made again. See takes and provenance.

Concept​

The first picture of a prop or character, drawn for 3D.

Turnaround​

A character drawn from the front, left, back and right, for building it in 3D.

Mesh, High poly​

The dense 3D model built from the concept or turnaround: Mesh on a prop, High poly on a character. An input for the Game mesh. It doesn't go in the game.

Game mesh​

The model rebuilt within your target look's budget, with its texture baked. The Retopo step (retopo).

Rig​

A model with a skeleton and skin weights, bones named for Godot's humanoid profile (skin).

Wearable​

A piece of clothing or armour made for one rigged character or base body, skinned to its skeleton.

Base body​

A plain body (underwear, a bodysuit, a mannequin) that a wardrobe of pieces fits onto.

Slot and layer​

Where a wardrobe piece goes (feet, outer torso) and how far out (0 skin-tight to 3 outer). A piece is fitted over the lower layers.

Outfit​

A named set of a body's pieces (gen outfit hero travel shirt coat boots).

Cloth chains​

Bone chains down what hangs (a cape, a skirt, long hair) that Godot's spring-bone simulator swings.

Sprite set​

A shot of an anime character in every expression, with talking and blinking frames.

2D rig​

A cut-out drawing split into parts on Godot bones (Skeleton2D), with idle, sway and head-tilt animations.

Round robin​

Several variants of one sound effect, so repeats don't sound the same.

Stems​

A track split into drums, bass, vocals and the rest.

Writing helper​

A small text model on your CPU behind the sparkle and dice buttons. See write better prompts.

VRAM​

The graphics card's memory. Most steps want most of it. See the VRAM scheduler.

MCP​

The Model Context Protocol: how Claude Code drives Gen Studio (gen-mcp). Its tools: MCP reference.
