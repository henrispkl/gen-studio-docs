The window in five minutes

Every part of the main window and what it's for, in the order you meet them. Read this once, then make something in First launch.

Four words first​

  • An asset is one thing you make: a prop, a character, a sound.
  • A step is one stage of making it: Concept, Mesh, Game mesh.
  • A take is one try at a step. Takes stay until you discard them.
  • The take in use is the one the next step builds from.

Use a different take, and the steps built from the old one turn stale: still there, but out of date. More in steps and paths.

The frame​

The top bar has the menus (File, Edit, View, Help), then Home and Assets, then one tab per open asset. At its right end are the project's name and Settings. The status bar along the bottom shows the running job, then CPU, RAM, GPU and VRAM, and Queue.

Home​

The app opens here. It answers two questions: what's happening, and what needs you?

  • The prompt bar makes something straight away. Pick a kind, write a line, pick a profile and press Make it. All kinds… opens the full list. You can also drop a picture, a model or a sound on it to import that file.
  • Latest takes shows the newest result of each asset. Click one to open it. Right-click for Open, Clone and Delete….
  • Waiting for you lists what can't go on without you: failures, reviews, stale steps. Each card has its fix. With nothing waiting it says "All clear".
  • On the GPU shows the running job, what's Up next and what's holding the VRAM.
  • Projects switches between your games. A project is a folder with a gen-project.toml in it. New project… asks for a folder and a Target look; Open folder… opens one you have.

Assets​

Every asset in the project. Press Assets in the top bar, or Ctrl+2. It's greyed out until a project is open.

  • The left side filters. All assets, then your groups, then Smart filters: Needs you, Working, Stale, Favourites and Not exported.
  • The cards are one per asset. The bar under each card has one segment per step, in the step's status colour. Click a card to see its steps on the right. Double-click to open it.
  • Right-click a card to open it, mark it a favourite, Re-run stale, Export to Godot, Show in folder, Clone or Delete….
  • The top row has the search, the sort (Last changed, Name, Kind), the card size, Find free assets and New asset.

New asset​

Ctrl+N, All kinds… on Home, or New asset on Assets.

Start from picks between From a description and From a file. The middle lists the kinds, each with a picture of what it makes; the search finds one by name. The right side shows Its steps, so you see the route before you start, then the Asset name, the Profile, the Seed and the kind's own fields. Generate queues the first step.

An asset's page​

This is where the work happens. Each open asset is a tab in the top bar.

The toolbar​

The asset's name, then what you do to the whole asset: Export to Godot, Auto-continue, Run to end and the ⋮ menu. Re-run stale shows only when something is stale. Continue shows when a step waits for your review. ⋮ has New clip and Wardrobe on a character, Clone asset and Delete asset…. Deleting moves the asset to the project's .trash folder.

The step pills​

The asset's steps, left to right, as pills. A dot on each shows its status. Click one to open it.

  • The dashed + at the end of a row opens Add step: what can follow, with the model each runs and about how long it takes.
  • Right-click a pill for Retake, Run to here, Branch from here…, Cancel its runs, Show in folder and Delete step….
  • A branch is a second row, named b1, b2. A character's clips have rows of their own.
  • An asset made from another one (a shot from its character) starts with that asset's pill, marked source. It's there to look at.

See add steps and paths.

The inputs form​

On the left: what the step builds from, then its form. Profile first, then the prompt and the step's own fields. Advanced shows the rest, and Presets. The fields are the ones in the steps reference.

The stage and its takes​

The picked take, large, in the middle. A picture, a 3D view you can turn, a waveform or text, depending on the step. Double-click it, or press Open large, for the big viewer.

  • The bar above it has the take's tools and one green button: New take or Retake, or Use and run with the next step's name, which uses the picked take and runs the step after it. Its ⋯ menu has Run next.
  • The strip below holds every take of this step, oldest first, ending with the New take card. The take in use is marked. Right-click a take for Use this take, Reroll (replace this take), a rating, compare and Discard….

A new take doesn't replace the one in use. You look, then choose. See retake and compare.

Details​

Details shows the facts of the file on the stage: the model, the seed, the time it took and the licence. On steps that change something you already have (Retouch, Edit part), the right side is a tool panel instead, and Details folds up at its foot.

When a step fails​

The step shows a panel that says what went wrong in plain words and what to do. Copy log copies the job's log. The common ones are in troubleshooting.

The queue​

Everything you start goes in the queue. One big model fits on the GPU at a time, so jobs wait their turn. Press Queue in the status bar.

Each row is a job and links to its step. Pause lets the running job finish and starts nothing new; Resume carries on. Downloads have their own lane beside the GPU job. See use the queue and the VRAM scheduler.

Settings​

Settings in the top bar, or Ctrl+,.

  • Models: what's installed and what fits your GPU. Install and remove here.
  • Project: this game's Target look (PS1 to PS4), Default profile, Auto-continue, and the sound format for export.
  • Storage: your libraries, the data folder, what uses the space, and Clean up for old takes.
  • Extensions: packs of steps and models, on and off. A change applies when you restart the app.
  • Downloads: your Civitai API key, for models whose creator asks for a login.
  • Appearance: theme, colours, text size and Reduce motion.
  • Setup: Open setup shows the first-run window again.
  • About: the version, the machine checks and licences.

Two of these shape every result. Target look is the era your game looks like and sets the budgets. Profile is how long a step may take and picks the model. See profiles and target looks.

Help​

Help → Help…, or F1. On a step, F1 opens that step's page.

The list has the step you're on, the asset kinds, the steps, the concepts, and troubleshooting and the glossary. What's new and Keyboard shortcuts are there too. Full guide on the website opens the same page here, with its pictures and clips. The Help menu also has Check this machine, which opens the checks in Settings → About.

Keys worth learning​

Ctrl+K searches every action and asset. On an asset, [ and ] move between steps, ← and → between takes, R makes a new take and U uses the picked take. All of them: keyboard shortcuts.
