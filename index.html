Every asset your game needs, made on your own GPU.

Gen Studio turns a sentence into concept art, 3D models, rigs, animation, textures, sound, voices and anime art, then puts them in your Godot project. Free and open source.

Get started See what it made

One sentence in. A character that walks out.

a young courier with short auburn hair, goggles on her forehead, a short teal jacket over a cream shirt, brown trousers, sturdy boots

  1. Write a sentenceThe Concept step draws her from your words. Don't like it? Retake; every try is kept.
  2. Four viewsThe same character from the front, both sides and the back, so the 3D step has no guessing to do.
  3. A game meshA model built from the views, then cut down to the triangle budget of the look you chose.
  4. PaintHer colours baked onto the low-poly mesh as one texture.
  5. Rig and animateA skeleton, skin weights, and a walk made from the words "walks forward at a steady pace".

Everything here came out of it.

Pictures, models, music and sound effects from one sample project, made on one graphics card. The ones with a button play.

What it makes

Each kind of asset has its own path of steps. Pick where you want to start.

Anime charactersA character you keep: her sheet, shots in any pose, expressions, stickers and a 2D rig. Props and modelsFrom a sentence, or your own picture, to a low-poly model in your target look's budget. Characters that moveConcept, four views, a game mesh, a skeleton, then clips. Textures, shaders, effectsSeamless tiles with their maps, shaders that compile, flipbooks. Sound and musicSound effects in variants, looping tracks, pieces composed bar by bar. Voices and wordsA voice from a description, dialogue with stable keys, item text as checked JSON. Pixel art and chiptuneTrue pixels on a limited palette, and music on a chosen sound chip.

The honest part

On your machinePrompts, pictures and results stay on your disk. No account, no credits, no one else's queue.
Nothing overwrittenA new try is a new take. Go back to any of them. What's out of date is marked.
Made again, the sameEvery take records its model revision, seed, settings and licence.
What you needLinux, an NVIDIA GPU (built and tested on 24 GB; smaller cards run the Fast profile), 80 to 358 GB of disk, Godot 4.7.
